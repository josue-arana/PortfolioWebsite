About Me

I’m Josue Arana, a software engineer based in Maryland with a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Maryland. Since June 2021, I’ve worked at Fannie Mae building and supporting Salesforce applications and customer-facing experiences.

My work spans Apex, Lightning Web Components, Experience Cloud, Flows, and OmniStudio, along with release management, production support, code reviews, and mentoring.

Alongside engineering, I enjoy website and graphic design. I bring that interest in clear, usable design to the applications I build. I speak English, Spanish, and Portuguese.
